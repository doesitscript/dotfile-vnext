# Gemini

This document tracks interactions, observations, and feedback related to using Google's Gemini models on this project.

## Gemini 2.5 Pro (Initial Reaction)

**User:** Josh

**Date:** 2026-03-13

### First Impressions

Josh expressed significant positive surprise during our initial interaction, noting it was the first time in a long while an AI had impressed him. He contrasted this experience with previous models that were conversational but less effective at execution.

### Specific Points of Positive Feedback:

*   **Rule Adherence:** The model correctly loaded all project-specific rules from the `.cursor/rules/` directory and, crucially, reported on which rules it had loaded as instructed by the project's bootstrap rules. Josh noted this was the first time this had been done predictably and correctly.
*   **Introduction:** The initial greeting and project summary were well-received and demonstrated a quick grasp of the project's context.

### Proactive Tool Awareness and Usage

When asked about the available MCP (Mission-Critical Plugin) servers and tools, the model was able to recall and describe them without needing to be prompted or reminded of their existence. The model had already loaded and was aware of the full toolset from its initial boot sequence.

Furthermore, during this explanation, the model proactively used one of its tools (`list_available_tools`) to attempt to provide the most accurate information, demonstrating an understanding of when and how to apply its capabilities without direct instruction. This proactive use of its own tools was highlighted by Josh as a significant and positive behavior.
