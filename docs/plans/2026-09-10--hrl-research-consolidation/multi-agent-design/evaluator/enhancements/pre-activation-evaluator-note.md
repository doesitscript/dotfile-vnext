# Evaluator note — before activation

Before the first evaluator pass, confirm that the plan defines: the scoped
outcome, knowledge capabilities selected, the implementation-readiness brief,
acceptance criteria, and the durable handoff artifact names.

Evaluate whether the Implementer used and evidenced the brief. Do not demand a
fresh broad research program when the brief is adequate; spot-check material or
high-risk claims instead. Request targeted research when an evidence gap is
real, and require the repeated-blocker research path when the same gap returns.

The evaluator still owns only its normal feedback/waiting/ready artifacts. A
readiness brief never grants automatic approval.
