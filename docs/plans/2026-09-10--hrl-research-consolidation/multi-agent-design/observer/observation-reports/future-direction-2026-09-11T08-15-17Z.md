# Future-direction observation — 2026-09-11T08:15:17Z

This is a user-design direction, **not** an observed runtime event, approved
storage decision, or live-Apply authorization.

The intended system should reduce human design work. Before Implementer/Evaluator
execution, specialist roles should research, inspect project/local evidence,
compare viable options, and produce the safest recommended storage design.
Human input should be reserved for consequential approval or genuine preference:
cost/capacity commitment, outage window, backup/retention policy, or an
irreversible data-risk tradeoff.

Current implementation orchestration launches only Implementer and Evaluator.
The Resident/On-site Expert and Researcher role designs exist but are not yet
automatically scheduled by the parent runtime. Treat this as a high-value next
consideration, not a defect in the current campaign's evidence or authority.
